Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

ChMS Ledger helps churches give budgets to ministry leaders, volunteers and missionaries and track the receipts spent against them. It is built so that the people who make ChMS Ledger never receive or store your church's financial data.

Where your data is kept

  • Budgets, expenses, receipt photos, approvals and the ledger are stored in your browser on your own device.
  • ChMS Ledger has no server and no central database. We cannot see, read, sell or share your data.
  • Reading text from receipt photos happens on your device. Photos are not uploaded anywhere for this.
  • When you share updates or send an invite link, the data goes only to the people you choose, through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or email).

Signing in with Google

Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose it, the app asks Google for:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, to show who you are in the app and to match you with the person your church invited.
  • Access to the app's own hidden folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission), to save and restore one backup file of your ChMS Ledger data. The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

Your backup lives in your own Google account. You can delete it at any time in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → ChMS Ledger → Delete hidden app data, and you can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

ChMS Ledger's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to sign you in and to store your own backup. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone else, and not read by people.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

ChMS Ledger has no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. It stores your settings (such as light or dark mode) on your device.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to More → Erase this device to remove everything stored on that phone or computer. To remove your Google Drive backup, follow the steps above.

Children

ChMS Ledger is meant for adults who manage church funds and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent through the ChMS Ledger issue page.
