What the app does

ChMS Ledger is a record-keeping tool. It helps track budgets, receipts, approvals and paybacks. It does not move money, make payments or hold funds, and it is not accounting, tax or legal advice.

Your responsibilities

  • Check that amounts, dates and receipts are correct, including details the app reads from receipt photos.
  • Keep backups. Data is stored on your device; if the device is lost and you have no Google Drive backup or backup file, the data cannot be recovered.
  • Only invite people your church has authorised, and keep invite links private: anyone with a link can see the budgets in it.
